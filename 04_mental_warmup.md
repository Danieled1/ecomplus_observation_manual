# 🧘‍♂️  Ecom+ Morning Mental Warm-Up  
*Date: 21 Oct 2025 — 10:55 AM | Duration: 5 minutes*

---

## 🔹 Step 1 – Reset (1 minute)
> *“Today isn’t about finishing code. It’s about seeing clearly.”*

- Close tabs and Slack notifications.  
- Open only VS Code / Notion and your `/observations` folder.  
- Breathe once in: *“Observe.”*  Breathe out: *“Don’t fix.”*  
  (Do this 3 times.)

---

## 🔹 Step 2 – Recall Core Intent (1 minute)
- We are mapping **current truth**, not proving effort.  
- The outcome is **clarity for Amit**, not perfection.  
- Every page you open should answer just three things:  
  1️⃣ Can it launch now?  
  2️⃣ What blocks it?  
  3️⃣ What can wait?  

---

## 🔹 Step 3 – Ground in Structure (1 minute)
Keep these **anchors** visible on a sticky note:

| Lens | Meaning |
|------|----------|
| ✅ Have | Works now |
| ❌ Missing | Planned but absent |
| ⚙️ Pending | Half-done / QA |
| 🕓 Post-Launch | Nice-to-have |

And remember:  
**Ready = works + consistent + recoverable.**

---

## 🔹 Step 4 – Page Observation Prep (1 minute)
Before you open a page, whisper to yourself:

> “One pass, one role, one lens at a time.”

1. Pick a page (Login, Dashboard, Lesson, etc.).  
2. Duplicate `02_page_lens_template.md`.  
3. Fill the four lens sections with bullets — not paragraphs.  
4. Stop once each lens has at least one line.  
5. Move on.  (Polish later if time remains.)

---

## 🔹 Step 5 – Perspective Shift (1 minute)
Imagine the meeting:

- Amit needs to *understand*, not *debug.*  
- Ben and Lior need *confidence* that it’s structured.*  
- You are the **guide**, not the defense.*

So focus on **clarity over detail.**  
Every “I don’t know” you write is a *sign of control,* not weakness.

---

## 🧩  When You Begin
- Open `student/login.md`.  
- Start typing your first “Have / Missing / Pending / Post-Launch.”  
- After 15 minutes you’ll already feel momentum.
