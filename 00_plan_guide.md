## 🔍  Four-Lens Check per Page

| Lens | Meaning |
|------|----------|
| **Have** | Implemented & working |
| **Missing** | Planned but absent |
| **Pending** | Half-done / QA needed |
| **Post-Launch** | Nice-to-have, not blocking |

---

## ✅  “Ready” Definition
A page = **Ready for launch** when:
- Works for all normal cases  
- Visuals consistent  
- No critical break on empty / error states  
- Remaining polish = cosmetic or enhancement

---

## 🧠  Focus Questions
- Can this page go live tomorrow without embarrassment?  
- What blocks it?  
- What can wait until after launch?

That’s it.  Finish those answers → you’re ready for Amit.
